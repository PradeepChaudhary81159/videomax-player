Privacy Policy

VideoMax Player  |  Last updated: November 07, 2026

Local-first design: VideoMax Player is a local video and music player. Media files and most settings stay on your device. We do not operate a media cloud for your library.

1. Introduction

VideoMax Player (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) is a local video and music player application for Android. We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

2. Information We Do Not Collect by Default

Unless required by an optional platform service you enable (see section 6), the App does not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Your media file contents or full media library for upload
  • Location data for tracking
  • Browsing history outside the App

Your videos, music, playlists, continue-watching positions, themes, and Private Vault data are stored on your device.

3. Permissions Used by the App

VideoMax Player may request the following permissions solely for core functionality:

Permission Purpose
Storage / Photos & videos / Music & audio To scan, list, and play media files stored on your device. Files are not uploaded to our servers.
Notifications (optional) Playback controls in the notification shade / lock screen media controls.
Internet (if declared in the app build) Only for optional features such as app updates via Google Play, or ads/premium billing if enabled in your installed version. Not used to upload your personal media library.

Why media access is needed: VideoMax Player is a local player. Access to photos/videos and audio is required only to discover and play files on the device.

4. Privacy Vault

The Privacy Vault is a local feature that can protect selected media with a PIN.

  • The PIN and vault association are stored only on your device.
  • We cannot access, recover, or view data inside your Privacy Vault.
  • If you forget your PIN and have not set a recovery method on the device, vault access may be lost.
  • Clearing app data / uninstalling can remove vault-related local data.

5. Local Data Storage

Settings, playlists, favorites, resume positions, and themes are stored locally (for example in app preferences or local storage). This data is not sent to a VideoMax cloud service operated by us.

6. Third-Party Services

Depending on the build you install from Google Play, the App may use Google Play services for distribution, updates, and (if enabled) in-app purchases or advertising.

  • Google Play / Play Billing: Purchase and license validation are handled by Google under Google’s policies.
  • Advertising (only if ads are present in your version): Ad partners (such as Google AdMob) may collect device and usage data according to their own privacy policies. You can limit ads personalization in your device Google settings.

We do not sell your media files. Review the permissions and Data safety section on the Google Play listing for the exact services in the published build.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is a general media utility. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents should supervise device media access permissions.

8. Data Security

Because your media library remains on the device, protection depends on your device lock screen, OS updates, and how you share the device. Keep your PIN and recovery options secure if you use Private Vault.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after changes means you acknowledge the updated policy.
